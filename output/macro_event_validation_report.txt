Question: does the Stage-9 stress composite rise at documented
real-world crises it was never told about? Event-study deltas
(mean of 8-week event window minus mean of the prior
12 weeks) are ranked against a placebo distribution of
168 non-event windows. DETECTED >= p90, PARTIAL >= p75.

Summary: 2/5 DETECTED, 0 PARTIAL.

Reading the verdicts: the composite is five US-AGGREGATE indicators
(diesel, truck spot rates, containerships at anchor, TSI,
inventory-to-sales), each scored CAUSALLY as a percentile rank of its
own history to that week (no full-series scaling), carried forward
weekly and 4-week smoothed. Every window is compared with placebo
windows from the same series.
  DETECTED  Suez Canal blockage: +0.135 (placebo p100), top driver Diesel_Sales_Prices — a 6-day foreign chokepoint that global schedules largely absorbed; a US signal here is container/spot-rate spillover.
  DETECTED  Red Sea shipping attacks: +0.039 (placebo p92), top driver Freight_Transportati — an Asia-Europe rerouting crisis whose cost landed mostly outside US lanes.
  NOT SEEN  COVID-19 pandemic onset: -0.087 (placebo p6) — US freight demand collapsed in Mar-Apr 2020: diesel and truck spot rates FELL while inventory-to-sales spiked, so a US freight-cost composite can read the onset as LOWER stress.
  NOT SEEN  US port congestion peak: +0.011 (placebo p72) — a domestic port event; containerships-at-anchor is the direct indicator, but that series only enters the causal composite from mid-2022.
  NOT SEEN  Russia invades Ukraine: -0.049 (placebo p18) — an energy-price shock atop already-elevated 2021-22 congestion, so the rise sits on a high base.
A US-macro lens sees what moves US freight prices and capacity; it is
not a global crisis detector, and the nulls above are reported as such.

Caveat: with 5 events this is external validity evidence, not a
powered statistical test. Placebo percentiles quantify how unusual
each rise is within THIS series; they are not p-values from an
independent sample.
