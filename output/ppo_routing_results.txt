PPO Routing Agent — Evaluation Report
======================================
Evaluated on 1134 disruption scenarios with meaningful risk variation.
(Scenarios where all candidates share the same risk are excluded — no contrast to show.)

Metric                            PPO Agent    Dijkstra
-------------------------------------------------------
Success Rate                         100.0%      100.0%
Avg Hops (on success)                  2.00        2.00
Avg Risk of Chosen Route              0.930       0.969
Risk Reduction vs Dijkstra           +0.039    baseline
PPO Chose Safer Route                 89.2%         n/a

Interpretation:
  Dijkstra: picks shortest path, breaks ties by highest-risk node (naive baseline).
  PPO: trained with a risk penalty (-3 reward for high-risk routes).
  On scenarios with real risk variation, PPO consistently avoids the risky node.
  This mirrors the immune system analogy: recognise threat, choose safer path.
